Tags: 
 -----
    ->Tags are pre-define  words which are enclosed with both opening and closing angular brackets 
    Ex: 
        <h1> , <meta> ,<title> , <p> , <figure> ,<li> ..etc 

    Types: 
        1. Paired tags 
            ->Any tags having opening and closings tags 
            Ex: 
                i. all heading tags 
                 Ex:   <h1>  </h1>
                       <h2>  </h2>
                       <h3>  </h3>
                       <h4>  </h4>
                       <h5>  </h5>
                       <h6>  </h6>

                ii. formatting tags : 
                    Ex: 
                        <i> </i>            ->italic
                        <em></em>           ->italic
                        <b></b>             ->bold
                        <strong></strong>   ->bold
                        <u></u>             ->underline
                        <strike></strike>   ->to strike text 
                        <sub></sub>         -> SubScript
                        <sup></sup>         -> Super Script
                        <mark></mark>       ->to Light text 
                        <center></center>   ->content at center 
                        <marquee></marquee> ->to scroll in different direction


              

    2. Unpaired tags/self closing tags 
    ->Any Tags having only opening tags 
            Ex: 
            <br> , <hr>, <img> , <col>, <input> ,<meta> ..etc 


Attribute : 
-----------
    ->Attribute are the additional information given to the tags 
    ->Attributes are always in opening 

    Ex: 
        <h1 align="center" id="heading"> </h1> 
         or 
        <img src="" alt="" height="" width="">


HTML Elements : 
----------- 
    ->opening , closing tag and the content together is called HTML Elements 

    Ex: <h1> Heading </h1>
        <p> ---------- </p>
